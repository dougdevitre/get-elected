## 2. Candidate call-time script (finance)

> Use during daily call time (`03-fundraising-plan.md`). Make the ask, then **stop talking.**

> "Hi [Name], it's Matt Grant — do you have two minutes? … I'm running for Congress here in the 2nd District, and I'm calling personally because I need your help.
>
> Here's the short version: costs are crushing families, our schools are stretched, and the only way we win this seat back in November is to nominate a Democrat who's actually won an election in this community — and that's me. I'm a teacher and a small-business owner. I sit on the school board. I show up.
>
> We're going to win this at the doors, not on TV — but that takes resources. **Can I count on you for $3,500 to get us through the August 4th primary?**"
>
> — *If yes:* "Thank you — that genuinely matters. I'll text you a link right now; can you do it today while we're on the phone?"
> — *If hesitant:* "I understand. Could you do $1,000?" *(keep stepping down)* "Even $250 puts literature in a canvasser's hand this weekend."
> — *If no:* "No problem at all — would you host a few neighbors for me, or send me two names who might help?"

> Follow up within 24 hours. Log every result for the treasurer (`tools/contribution-tracker.md`).

# First 30 Days Checklist

The first 30 days set whether a campaign is real or a wish. This checklist sequences the legal, financial, and organizational moves that have to happen before public launch. Work top to bottom.

## Week 1 — Make It Legal & Real

- [ ] Decide, on paper, **why you're running** (one paragraph) and your **win number**
- [ ] Recruit a **treasurer** (cannot be skipped — they own compliance)
- [ ] File **FEC Form 2** (Statement of Candidacy) within 15 days of qualifying
- [ ] File **FEC Form 1** (Statement of Organization) for the committee
- [ ] Open a **dedicated campaign bank account**
- [ ] Reserve **name, domain, email, and social handles**
- [ ] Draft the **"Paid for by" disclaimer** for all materials

## Week 2 — Money & Message

- [ ] Build and **rate the prospect list** (capacity × inclination)
- [ ] Write the **core message** (3 priorities) and **stump speech**
- [ ] Set the **fundraising goal** and weekly pace
- [ ] Schedule **call time** blocks (2–3 hrs/day) — and protect them
- [ ] Line up **3–5 founding donors** before going public
- [ ] Confirm **contribution limits** and intake process with the treasurer

## Week 3 — Build the Team & Plan

- [ ] Recruit a **kitchen cabinet** (5–7 trusted advisors)
- [ ] Identify a **campaign manager / lead volunteer**
- [ ] Draft the **campaign plan** (timeline, budget, vote goal)
- [ ] Set up **data**: voter file access, tracker spreadsheets, CRM
- [ ] Create **launch collateral**: palm card, yard sign, logo, website

## Week 4 — Launch

- [ ] Finalize and distribute the **launch press release**
- [ ] Schedule the **kickoff event** (venue, program, turnout plan)
- [ ] Stand up **social channels** with consistent branding
- [ ] Recruit the **first volunteer shifts** (doors/phones)
- [ ] Confirm the **first reporting deadline** is on the calendar with alarms
- [ ] **Go public.** Announce, then immediately ask for money and volunteers.

## The 30-Day Test

If, at day 30, you have a **treasurer, a bank account, filed paperwork, a message, a prospect list, and 3 founding donors**, you have a campaign. If not, fix that before anything else.

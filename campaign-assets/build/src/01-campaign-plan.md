# Campaign Plan

This plan translates the campaign's goals into a numbers-driven path to victory. Every section below should be revisited monthly and after every reporting deadline. Figures in brackets are placeholders to confirm with your data vendor and county clerk.

## 1. The Win Number

The win number is the number of votes required to win, derived from expected turnout — not from registration totals.

| Input | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Expected turnout (this election type) | [TURNOUT] | County clerk + past 3 comparable elections |
| Win threshold | 50% + 1 (or top-two for primary) | Election rules |
| **Win number** | **[WIN #]** | Turnout × threshold |
| Cushion target (+5%) | [WIN # × 1.05] | Buffer for error |

> **Rule of thumb:** Build your entire voter-contact program backward from the win number, not forward from your volunteer list.

## 3. Strategic Pillars

1. **A disciplined, repeated message** — lower costs, stronger schools, protect Social Security & Medicare.
2. **Direct voter contact at scale** — doors and phones are the highest-ROI persuasion channels.
3. **An earned-media engine** — a steady cadence of local press and a credible candidate story.
4. **A funded operation** — enough cash to execute the field and mail plan without scrambling.

## 4. Timeline & Phases

| Phase | Window | Primary objective |
|---|---|---|
| Build | Now → 120 days out | Infrastructure, money, list, volunteers |
| Persuade | 120 → 30 days out | ID + persuade swing voters |
| Mobilize (GOTV) | Final 30 days | Turn out IDed supporters |
| Report & close | Post-election | Final reports, thank-yous, debt retirement |

## 5. Budget Frame

| Category | Target % of budget |
|---|---|
| Voter contact (mail, digital, doors) | 50–60% |
| Staff / consultants | 15–25% |
| Fundraising costs | 5–10% |
| Compliance / admin / overhead | 5–10% |
| Reserve | 5% |

## 6. Key Metrics Dashboard (update weekly)

- Cash on hand vs. burn rate
- Voter IDs collected vs. goal
- Volunteer shifts filled vs. needed
- Donor count and average gift
- Earned-media hits

## 7. Risks & Contingencies

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Fundraising shortfall | Tiered budget; cut paid media before field |
| Opposition attack | Pre-written rapid-response (see Crisis Plan) |
| Low volunteer turnout | Paid canvass backstop budgeted |
| Compliance miss | Calendar with 2-week lead alarms (see Compliance Calendar) |

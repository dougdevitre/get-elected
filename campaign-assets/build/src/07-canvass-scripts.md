# Canvass & Phone Scripts

Scripts keep voter contact consistent, on-message, and fast to capture as data. Train every volunteer on these. The goal at the door is not a speech — it's an **ID** (where does this voter stand?) and, for supporters, a **commitment**.

## Door Script — Persuasion / ID

> **Intro:** "Hi, I'm [NAME], a volunteer with Matt Grant's campaign for Congress. Do you have a quick second?"
>
> **The ID question:** "We're reaching out to neighbors before the [ELECTION]. On a scale of 1 to 5, how likely are you to support Matt Grant — a teacher and small-business owner running to lower costs, fund our schools, and protect Social Security and Medicare?"
>
> **If 4–5 (Supporter):** "That's great to hear. Can Matt count on your vote on [DATE]?" → *Mark Supporter. Offer yard sign / volunteer ask.*
>
> **If 3 (Undecided):** "Totally fair. What matters most to you this election?" → *Listen. Connect ONE issue to the platform. Leave palm card.*
>
> **If 1–2 (Opposed):** "I appreciate your time — have a good one." → *Mark and move on. Never argue.*
>
> **Close (everyone):** "Thanks so much. Here's a card with more info." 

### Door Data to Capture
- Support level (1–5)
- Top issue (undecideds)
- Yard sign? Volunteer? Email/phone?
- Not home → leave lit, mark for retry

## Phone Script — ID / GOTV

> "Hi, may I speak with [VOTER]? My name is [NAME] and I'm a volunteer for Matt Grant for Congress. I'll be brief —
>
> **(ID)** Can Matt count on your support in the [ELECTION] on [DATE]?
>
> **(If yes / GOTV phase)** Wonderful — do you know where your polling place is? Do you have a plan for what time you'll vote? … Great, we'll see you at the polls!
>
> **(If undecided)** No problem — what's the most important issue to you right now? … Thanks, I'll make sure you get more information.
>
> Thanks for your time, and have a great day."

## Text Message Templates

- **ID:** "Hi [NAME], it's [VOL] with Matt Grant for Congress. Can Matt count on your vote on [DATE]? Reply Y/N/Maybe. Reply STOP to opt out."
- **GOTV:** "[NAME], polls are open until [TIME] today. Your polling place: [LOCATION]. Need a ride? Reply RIDE. — Team Grant"

## Volunteer Reminders
- Be warm, brief, and honest. You represent the candidate.
- Never argue, never mislead, never guess at facts — say "I'll find out."
- Respect "no soliciting" signs and no-contact requests.
- Log everything the same day.

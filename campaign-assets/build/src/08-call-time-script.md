# Call Time Script & Caller Card

Finance call time is the candidate's single highest-value activity. This is the script and the per-prospect card that make each hour productive. The candidate makes these calls — they cannot be delegated.

## The Five-Part Ask

1. **Connect** — "Hi [NAME], it's Matt Grant. Did I catch you at an okay time?"
2. **Why I'm running** — one or two sentences, the core message.
3. **Why I'm calling you specifically** — the personal connection.
4. **The specific ask** — name a number.
5. **The close** — confirm logistics, say thank you.

## Script

> "Hi [NAME], it's Matt Grant — how are you? … I won't keep you long.
>
> As you may have heard, I'm running for Congress here in [DISTRICT]. I'm a teacher and a small-business owner, and I'm running to lower the cost of living, fund our schools, and protect Social Security and Medicare.
>
> I'm calling you personally because [SHARED CONNECTION / why they'll care]. To run a serious campaign, I need to raise [GOAL] by [DATE] — and I'm hoping I can count on you for **$3,300**, the maximum for the primary.
>
> *(Silence. Let them answer.)*
>
> **If yes:** "Thank you — that genuinely means a lot. The easiest way is [LINK/method]. Can I send that over right now while we're on the phone?"
>
> **If hesitant:** "I completely understand. Would [$1,000 / $500] work for you today?"
>
> **If no/later:** "No problem at all. Can I follow up with you on [DATE]? … Thank you for taking my call."

## Call Time Rules

- **Pre-rate the list:** capacity × inclination. Call best prospects first.
- **Ask for a specific number**, then stop talking.
- **Always offer to take it now** (link/text while on the call).
- **Log immediately.** A pledge is not money until it clears.
- **Protect the hours** — 2–3 hours/day, scheduled, no exceptions.

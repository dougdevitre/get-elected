## 3. Federal Contribution Limits (2025–2026 cycle)

| Donor | To this campaign (per election) |
|---|---|
| Individual | $3,300 (primary) + $3,300 (general) |
| Multicandidate PAC | $5,000 per election |
| Party committee | $5,000 per election (+ coordinated expenditures) |
| Corporation / union (direct) | Prohibited |

> Primary and general are **separate elections**, each with its own limit. Undesignated gifts apply to the next election. If you lose the primary, general-election money must be refunded, redesignated, or reattributed within the rules.

## 4. Call Time Discipline

Call time is the engine. Treat it like a scheduled, non-negotiable shift.

- **Block 2–3 hours/day**, minimum, in the build phase.
- Work from a **rated, researched list** (capacity × inclination), best prospects first.
- **Ask for a specific number.** "Can I count on you for $3,300?" beats "whatever you can do."
- Log every call same-day; pledges aren't money until the check clears.
- A **caller card** for every prospect: name, capacity, connection, past giving, ask amount.

# Filing & Compliance Calendar

This is a federal compliance reference for a U.S. House campaign. It lists the core FEC registrations, the reporting schedule, and special-notice rules. **Exact dates change every cycle** — pull your campaign's specific deadlines from the FEC's official reporting schedule and enter them in the table below.

## Registration (one-time, at the start)

| Form | What it is | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **FEC Form 2** | Statement of Candidacy | Within 15 days of becoming a candidate (raising/spending over $5,000) |
| **FEC Form 1** | Statement of Organization (the committee) | Within 10 days of the committee being designated |
| Bank account | Dedicated campaign depository | Before accepting funds |
| Treasurer | Named, trained, responsible for filings | Before accepting funds |

## Regular Reporting (House campaigns)

House committees in an election year generally file on a **quarterly** schedule, plus pre- and post-election reports.

| Report | Covers | Typical due |
|---|---|---|
| April Quarterly | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | Apr 15 |
| July Quarterly | Apr 1 – Jun 30 | Jul 15 |
| October Quarterly | Jul 1 – Sep 30 | Oct 15 |
| Pre-Primary | through 20 days before primary | 12 days before primary |
| Pre-General | through 20 days before general | 12 days before general |
| Post-General | through 20 days after general | 30 days after general |
| Year-End | Oct 1 – Dec 31 | Jan 31 |

## Special Notices

| Notice | Rule |
|---|---|
| **48-Hour Notice** | Contributions of $1,000+ received after the 20th day but more than 48 hours before an election must be reported within 48 hours |
| **Excessive contribution** | Refund, redesignate, or reattribute within **30 days** of the treasurer's receipt |
| **Electioneering / disclaimers** | "Paid for by" disclaimers required on public communications and paid digital ads |

## Recordkeeping Standard

- Itemize contributions/expenditures over **$200** aggregate (name, address, occupation, employer).
- Keep records for **3 years**.
- Track primary vs. general limits separately per donor.
- Reconcile the bank account to reported cash-on-hand every report.

## Two-Week Lead Alarms

For every deadline above, set a reminder **14 days** and **3 days** prior. Late or inaccurate reports are the most common — and most avoidable — source of FEC penalties.

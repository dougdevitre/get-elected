# Join the Team

This is a recruitment handout and sign-up frame for new volunteers — hand it out at events, the door, and house parties. The ask is specific: pick a role, pick a shift, show up.

## Why It Matters

Campaigns are won by people, not ads. A volunteer who knocks 30 doors reaches more persuadable voters in an afternoon than most TV buys reach in a neighborhood. **You are the campaign.**

## What Matt Stands For

- **Lower the cost of living** — make work pay again.
- **Fund our public schools** — every kid gets a real shot.
- **Protect Social Security & Medicare** — a promise kept.

## Pick Your Role

| Role | Time | What you'll do |
|---|---|---|
| **Canvasser** | 2–3 hrs | Knock doors, talk to neighbors |
| **Phone/Text banker** | 1–2 hrs | Reach voters from home |
| **Host** | One evening | Throw a house party for friends |
| **Data/Office** | Flexible | Enter data, prep packets |
| **Visibility** | 1 hr | Sign waving, parades, tabling |
| **Driver** | Election Day | Get supporters to the polls |

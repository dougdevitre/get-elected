## Teacher and Small-Business Owner Matt Grant Launches Campaign for Congress in Missouri's 2nd District

**[CITY], MO —** Matt Grant, a former public-school teacher and local small-business owner, today announced his campaign for the U.S. House of Representatives in Missouri's 2nd Congressional District, pledging a campaign focused on lowering the cost of living, strengthening public schools, and protecting Social Security and Medicare.

"Families here are working harder than ever and still falling behind," Grant said. "I'm running because the people who do the work deserve a representative who shows up and does theirs. Lower costs, stronger schools, and a promise kept on Social Security and Medicare — that's what this campaign is about."

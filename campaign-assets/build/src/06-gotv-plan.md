# Get-Out-the-Vote (GOTV) Plan

GOTV is the final-stretch operation that converts identified supporters into cast ballots. Persuasion is over; this is pure turnout. The plan below covers the last [4] days plus early/absentee voting.

## Principle

> You do not persuade during GOTV. You only turn out voters you have already identified as supporters. Every contact is a reminder, a plan, and a removal of friction.

## The Universe

| Tier | Who | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | IDed supporters who vote irregularly | Highest — the whole ballgame |
| 2 | IDed supporters who vote occasionally | High |
| 3 | Strong leans not yet confirmed | Medium |
| (Exclude) | Opponents and reliable opposition | Never contact |

## Channels & Cadence (final 96 hours)

| When | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|
| GOTV − 4 days | Mail / door hanger | "Your polling place + hours" |
| GOTV − 2 days | Phone / text | "Do you have a plan to vote?" |
| Election Day AM | Text / call | "Polls are open — here's your location" |
| Election Day midday | Door knock (Tier 1) | Ride/relief offer, friction removal |
| Election Day PM | Call (not-yet-voted) | "Polls close at [TIME] — go now" |

## The Vote Plan (ask every contact)

1. **When** will you vote?
2. **What time** of day?
3. **How** will you get there?
4. **Where** is your polling place? (Tell them.)

People who make a specific plan are dramatically more likely to follow through.

## Early & Absentee

- Track who has already voted and **remove them from the list** immediately to avoid wasting contacts.
- Chase outstanding absentee ballots: "Have you mailed it? Need help returning it?"
